Installations

Was Here: From Presence to Trace

2025 · Concept · Algorithms · Fabrication

An interactive projection installation in an RMIT corridor. Four frames trace how a passing body registers in digital systems — a live silhouette, a data trail, a mark on the environment, and finally an anonymous flow of information. Nobody has to stop or press anything; walking through is the whole interaction.

Telepathic Jar

2025 · Concept · Hardware · Firmware

A pair of IoT bottles that sense the weather where each person is and translate it into light and mist. Instead of showing raw sensor readings, it curates them — colour, brightness and fog become a quiet way to feel that someone far away is having a grey afternoon. Built on ESP32 with MQTT over TLS.

TOUCH IT!

2024 · Hardware · Software · UX

A passive, battery-free accessory that moves your finger off the screen. Borrowing the layout of a kalimba, metal tines act as extended electrodes so taps and swipes register without your hand covering what you are trying to read. The companion iOS prototype is written in Swift, with a threshold that separates a tap from a drag.

Dislocation Communication

2024 · UX Design · Game Design · Research

A two-player narrative game for parents and children in single-parent families, built on the Satir family therapy model. One player describes a real conflict; the other uses an "iceberg searchlight" to read the layers of feeling underneath it. Repairing the room is how the game marks that you understood each other.
