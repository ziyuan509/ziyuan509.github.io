Play Notes

Not reviews. What I took away from each one, as someone who makes games.

O
Outer Wilds PC · 2019 · played 2026.07 · Exploration · Narrative · Time loop
It takes progress out of the save file entirely and puts it in your head. The twenty-two minute loop resets the world but never resets your understanding. The bravest part is that it will let you sit stuck for hours with no prompt at all, on the assumption that you can find your own way out — and it is right.
takeawayPut growth in the player's understanding rather than the character's stats and you no longer need a tutorial system at all.
B
Baba Is You PC / Switch · 2019 · played 2026.05 · Puzzle · Rule systems
The rules themselves are objects you can push. That sentence is short, but it means every level teaches you to re-read something you just learned. What I keep coming back to is how it handles being stuck: the map is open, so you can walk away, and the puzzle is often solved by the time you come back.
takeawayLetting a player shelve a puzzle protects the moment of solving it better than any hint button does.
R
Return of the Obra Dinn PC · 2018 · played 2026.02 · Deduction · Information design
Its verification rule is the example I reach for most often in user research: it confirms your answers only in batches of three. That number sits at exactly the right place — large enough to block brute force, small enough that you still get feedback while you are on the right track. A single numeric decision carries the whole game's sense of trust.
takeawayThe granularity of feedback is a design variable, not an implementation detail.
T
Tunic PC · 2022 · played 2025.11 · Action adventure · Manual
Making the instruction manual a collectible is executed frighteningly well. But late in the game I did feel the friction: once not understanding stops being fun and starts being work, there is no path to step down. I bounced off twice and only came back the third time with a walkthrough open — probably a cost it chose deliberately.
takeawayDeliberate obscurity needs a relief valve, or it filters out more than just impatient players.
C
Chants of Sennaar PC · 2023 · played 2025.08 · Puzzle · Language
Filling in a notebook is the verification mechanic, which forces your hypotheses to become explicit. That step matters: because the guess is written down, the system can check it and you can go back and look at it. As a researcher I half want to lift this interaction straight into an interview tool.
takeawayHave the player write the guess down and it stops being a mental state and becomes an object you can interact with.
