Pacing the Tutorial: How Information Density Shapes Early Player UnderstandingBest Paper Honorable Mention

Ziyuan Qu, A. Collaborator, Someone Advisor
CHI · 2026
pdfdoicodevideo
abs
bib
Tutorials are often treated as a single delivery of information, but player understanding is built in batches. In a 32-participant study we compare three pacing schemes and measure their effect on early input accuracy and subjective load. Splitting the same amount of information into more, shorter batches significantly lowers cognitive load, at the cost of a longer overall time to proficiency. We propose a simple model relating batch size to the mastery curve.

Handing Back Complexity: A Study of Expert Workflows in Level Editors

B. Coauthor, Ziyuan Qu, Someone Advisor
UIST · 2025

Level editors tend to trade approachability against sufficiency. We interviewed 14 senior level designers about how they route around the simplifying assumptions their tools make, and how they take complexity back into their own hands. We report four recurring workaround patterns.
